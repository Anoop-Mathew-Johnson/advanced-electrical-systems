# Advanced Electrical Systems

## Experimental Electrical Engineering Research

## Overview

This repository documents experimental and theoretical research concepts in advanced electrical engineering.

The objective is to investigate unconventional approaches to electrical machines, transformers, magnetic systems and power-system technologies.

## Research Areas

Current areas of interest include:

* Transformer optimisation
* Magnetic systems
* Active Magnetic Bearings
* Power conversion
* Electrical-machine design
* Electromagnetic systems
* Energy efficiency
* Advanced electrical architectures

## Current Research Concepts

### Möbius-Inspired Transformer

An exploratory concept investigating whether alternative winding geometries inspired by Möbius topology could produce useful electrical or magnetic characteristics.

Research questions include:

* How would the winding geometry affect magnetic flux?
* Could the topology influence leakage inductance?
* What effect could the geometry have on losses?
* How would manufacturing constraints affect feasibility?

### Möbius-Inspired Active Magnetic Bearing

An exploratory concept investigating whether unconventional electromagnetic geometries could provide useful characteristics for active magnetic-bearing systems.

### Advanced Electrical Control Systems

Research into alternative electromagnetic and control architectures for improving efficiency, controllability or system performance.

## Important Note

The concepts in this repository are **research hypotheses**, not claims of validated technological breakthroughs.

Any proposed performance improvement must be demonstrated through appropriate modelling, simulation or experimental evidence.
